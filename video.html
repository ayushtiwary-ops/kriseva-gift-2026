How it was made, since we are asked to be honest about method. Every frame is a real screenshot of the running product, captured headless at 3200 by 1800 from the live build, not a mockup. Every shot is cropped to the element the narration is actually talking about, so the picture changes with each sentence rather than sitting still. 27 shots, averaging four seconds. The narration is synthesised speech running locally on Kokoro, and every caption is the exact sentence being spoken, held for exactly that sentence’s own audio, so picture and voice cannot drift. The figures spoken are the measured ones in measured results.
