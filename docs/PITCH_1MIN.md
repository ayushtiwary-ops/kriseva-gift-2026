# PITCH, 1 MINUTE, ROUND 2 GRAND JURY

Status: STABLE. Rewritten 2026-08-19 against the simplified build.

Round 2 is a **1 minute pitch and 4 minutes of Q&A** in front of the Grand Jury,
for the Top 20. They have already seen Round 1 or been briefed on it.

**Word budget: about 150 words.** That is four short paragraphs. Do not try to
fit the Round 1 pitch into a third of the time; say fewer things properly.

---

## The script

> "A fund in GIFT City files four numbers to the regulator every quarter. Those
> numbers come from five different parties who close their books at different
> times, so they disagree, and nothing tells you that.
>
> Every system built for this picks one. **Ours refuses to.** It pins every number
> to the exact line it came from, names why the sources disagree, and hands the
> decision to a person who signs for it. A silent pick is the only error nobody
> downstream can catch.
>
> Nine steps, four use a model, five are plain code. **The steps that decide
> whether a number survives have no model in them**, because a model cannot check
> a model. Twenty-four out of twenty-four planted failures caught. Zero silent
> picks. A full case in about five seconds.
>
> We built it from published circulars with no data and no customers. **Everything
> still missing is something only access can fix.** Sixty days at GIFT City with
> twelve introductions and real documents, and we come back with the base rate
> nobody has measured."

## The four questions most likely in the 4 minutes, and the one-line answers

**"Why can't a good model just do this?"**
> "We tested that and it did fine. What no prompt gave us is a named decider, a
> written reason, a second signature, and the same answer eleven months later."

**"Your data is fake."**
> "All of it, and every document says so on its face. It proves the pipeline does
> what the design says on ten failure shapes. It does not predict a real return,
> and that is the first thing we would measure with access."

**"What is not built?"**
> "Per-field regulatory rule mapping. It says so on the screen. We will not
> invent a citation, and it is ask four of the residency."

**"Why you two?"**
> "We removed two models from our own system this week because they were adding
> risk without adding judgement, and we published the before and after. That
> discipline is the product."
