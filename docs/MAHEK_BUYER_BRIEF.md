## 4. What every other system does

Given two numbers, a normal system files one. The ordinary rule, take the figure
most documents agree on, files **17,800,000** here.

**That is the wrong number, and nothing announces it.**

Say this exactly, because it is the sharpest thing we own:

> "A silent pick is the only error nobody downstream can catch, because there is
> no disagreement left to find."

---

## 5. What we do instead, in four moves

1. **We refuse to pick.** Where the documents disagree, the field stays open.
2. **Every number is pinned to the exact line it came from**, with who wrote it
   and the moment it was true.
3. **A named person decides in writing**, and a second person, who cannot be the
   first, confirms. In the demo Priya decides and Rajiv Menon confirms.
4. **It seals.** Change one byte and the chain breaks visibly.

And the one field that is different: **complaints closed appears in no document at
all.** So it cannot be decided. It can only be **attested**, by a named person
putting their name to a number with no source, and it is marked that way forever.
