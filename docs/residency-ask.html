WHAT WE NEED FROM THE RESIDENCY, IN NUMBERS

Status: STABLE. Written 2026-08-19. Every ask below names what it unblocks, what we produce with it, and how we will know it worked. Nothing here asks for money.

The residency is 1 September to 30 October 2026, 60 days, onsite at GIFT City, with a stipend of Rs 1,00,000 per team. We are not asking for more of that. We are asking for the four things money cannot buy from outside, and all four are access.

1. Why the ask is access and not capital

Read our own honest list and the pattern is immediate.

Every gap in the right column is an access problem, not an engineering problem. Two more months of building without access widens the left column and closes none of the right. That is why the residency is the correct next step and more runway is not.

2. The four asks, in exact numbers

Ask 1. Twelve introductions to Fund Management Entities

Number: 12 warm introductions, of the 217 FMEs registered in GIFT IFSC.

That is 5.5 percent of the market. We are asking to meet one entity in eighteen.

What it unblocksThe base rate. How often do sources actually disagree on a real return, and on which fields
Why twelveWe plan on roughly two thirds converting to a real conversation. Eight discovery interviews is the point where we stop hearing new failure modes, and below six we would be generalising from anecdotes
What we produceA written base-rate study: conflicts per return, by field and by cause, across the entities that will talk to us. We publish the method whether or not the answer suits us
How we know it workedEight completed interviews by 30 September
The result that would hurt usIf conflicts turn out to be rare and trivial, our thesis is wrong. We would rather find that out in sixty days than after eighteen months

Ask 2. Five real document sets, redacted, four quarters each

Number: 5 entities x 4 quarters = 20 quarter-sets, roughly 80 to 100 documents, with every name and figure redacted or scrambled by the entity before we see it.

We do not need real numbers. We need real shapes.

What it unblocksThe gap between our corpus and reality. Ours is plain text and machine clean. A real one is PDF, Excel, Word and scanned. The DRR tender itself requires multi-format submission support
Why five and why four quartersOne quarter shows a document. Four shows whether the same disagreement recurs, which is the whole structural claim. Five entities is the smallest sample that can show a pattern is not one administrator's habit
What we produceAn extraction accuracy measurement on real document formats, published with its failure cases. A published 2025 benchmark put the best multimodal model at about 96 percent on clean invoices and about 87 percent on scanned receipts. We expect to be worse than our synthetic results and we will publish the number we get
How we know it worked20 quarter-sets in hand by 15 October, and a measured accuracy figure on real formats by 30 October
What we will signAny NDA the entity's counsel writes. Data stays on their infrastructure if they prefer

Ask 3. Twenty hours with Compliance Officers and Principal Officers

Number: 20 hours total, across at least 6 named officers, in blocks of 90 minutes.

The Compliance Officer and the Principal Officer are both mandated by IFSCA, both required to be based in the IFSC, and the signature on the return is personal. They are the buyer, the user and the person carrying the risk, and we have never met one.

What it unblocksWhether the product is usable by the person who signs, and whether personal signature risk is the real purchase driver we believe it is
Why twenty hoursSix officers at roughly three sessions each. Enough to watch the same person use it twice and see what they stopped doing
What we produceA usability record: every place an officer hesitated, every word they had to ask about, and what they did instead of what we designed. Our glossary is 32 terms long because we assumed they would need it. We may be wrong in both directions
How we know it workedSix officers have each completed a real quarterly field end to end in the product by 30 October

Ask 4. One working session with IFSCA supervision on the rule mapping

Number: 1 session, 2 hours, on which regulation requires which reported field.

This is the smallest ask and the one that changes the product most.

What it unblocksThe single largest missing feature. Per-field regulatory rule mapping is not built, and it is the difference between "these numbers disagree" and "this number is required by this rule and it disagrees"
Why we have not built itBecause we will not invent a citation. We can read the circulars. We cannot confirm from outside which rule the supervisor considers authoritative for each field, and a wrong citation in a compliance product is worse than an absent one
What we produceThe mapping, built and shipped, with each field carrying the rule it answers to. Published back for any entity to use, whether or not they are our customer
How we know it workedEvery one of the four reported fields carries a sourced rule reference by 30 October, and the "not built" notice comes off the screen honestly

4. Why the timing is now and not next year

Three dated facts, all sourced in FACT_CARD.md, all of which stop being advantages if we wait.

  1. **The fiduciary segregation circular of 10 April 2026 requires existing

schemes to comply by 30 September 2026. One firm can no longer be fiduciary and administrator and valuer and auditor. More independent firms means more overlapping records per fund, which means more disagreement per return. That deadline falls inside the residency.** We would be measuring the change as it happens rather than reading about it afterwards.

  1. **The DRR solution was awarded in January 2026, and pre-bid responses state 18

months to final go-live**, so the earliest plausible date is the second half of 2027, with entity migration after that. At least six more quarterly cycles get filed the old way. The window where entity-side evidence integrity matters is open now and it is not open indefinitely.

  1. **None of the ten named DRR modules is lineage, provenance, source-document

evidence, or reconciliation to a golden source.** DRR is the regulator's front door. What we do sits on the entity side of it, before the filing is made. Those are complements, and we would rather establish that with the supervisor present than argue it later.

5. What we are not asking for

Said plainly, because an ask list is judged as much by what it leaves out.

  • Not money beyond the stated stipend. The engineering is not the constraint.
  • Not an endorsement. No entity, customer or regulator relationship may be

implied from a residency place, and we will not imply one.

  • Not preferential regulatory treatment. If we reach the sandbox we want to

enter it on the published criteria under the FinTech Sandbox Framework 2026.

  • Not introductions we would waste. If the first three FME conversations say

the problem is not real, we will report that and stop asking for the other nine.
