### Rail

**Nearest useful stations:**
- **Gandhinagar Capital (GNC)**, the closer of the two: approximately 10-15 km from GIFT City, 20-30 minutes by taxi or auto-rickshaw (sources vary within this range; retrieved 2026-08-18). Better target if a direct or connecting train serves it.
- **Ahmedabad Junction (Kalupur, ADI)**, more trains serve it but it's farther: approximately 20-35 km depending on route, 35-60 minutes by taxi in normal traffic (retrieved 2026-08-18). Alternative last-mile option: the Ahmedabad Metro runs directly from Kalupur to a GIFT City station, about 24.3 km, roughly 44 minutes, fare Rs 23-25, with three line changes (Blue to Old High Court, Red to Motera Stadium, Yellow to GNLU, Violet to GIFT City), operating 06:15 to 22:20 (yometro.com, retrieved 2026-08-18). Cheap and easy to evidence for reimbursement, but slow and change-heavy with two people and full luggage; better suited to a light-bag daytime trip than a Thursday-evening arrival.

**Journey shape:** a long-haul train (Rajdhani, Duronto, or Vande Bharat class) into Ahmedabad or Gandhinagar Capital, then a taxi for the last leg. Illustrative data point for a Case C origin (not a claim about `<ORIGIN CITY>`): the Mumbai Central to Gandhinagar Capital Vande Bharat Express (train 20901/20902) covers 522 km, departs Mumbai Central 06:00, runs six days a week except Wednesday, AC Chair Car and Executive Chair Car only. Sources disagree on total journey time, roughly 5 to 6 hours across different listings (retrieved 2026-08-18); confirm the exact scheduled arrival on IRCTC before booking.

**What to book:** the train, then a taxi or app cab for the station-to-hotel leg, booked separately. Do not assume a taxi rank will be well-stocked at a smaller station like GNC in the evening.

**Booking window risk:** today is Tuesday 18 August, departure is Thursday evening 20 August, roughly 48 hours of notice. Tatkal booking on IRCTC opens the day before travel; confirm the exact opening time directly on IRCTC, as this rule has changed before and should not be assumed. Outside Tatkal, popular routes and premium trains (Vande Bharat, Shatabdi, Rajdhani) commonly show waitlists or dynamic/Flexi-fare pricing this close to departure. Check tonight.

**Reimbursement implication:** generally the cheapest mode, and the easiest to keep near the Rs 5,000 per-team cap for two people if the distance is moderate. The IRCTC e-ticket and payment confirmation double as the receipt automatically; download the PDF for offline access.

### Air

**Gateway:** Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel International Airport (AMD), Ahmedabad.

**Distance and transfer to GIFT City:** sources disagree meaningfully here, from about 12 km (Wikipedia) to roughly 30 km (several travel-guide sources), and travel time from about 20 minutes up to an hour depending on traffic and source. Treat 20-25 km and 30-45 minutes as the working planning number, and re-check on a live maps app closer to travel time, since this corridor's traffic varies a lot by time of day. Could not fully reconcile the source disagreement (retrieved 2026-08-18).

**Journey shape:** flight into AMD, then a prepaid airport taxi or app cab (Uber and Ola both list 24/7 airport service per their own site pages) to the hotel or venue.

**What to book:** the flight, then decide the airport transfer separately. A prepaid taxi counter is the safer choice on arrival night versus locating an app cab in an unfamiliar terminal.

**Booking window risk:** the highest of the three modes. A same-week Delhi-Ahmedabad fare search on 2026-08-18 showed one-way prices roughly Rs 4,000 to 9,000+ per person depending on airline, day, and exact search time, well above the levels typically quoted for bookings made two or more weeks out. This is an illustrative data point for a Case B distant-metro origin, not a claim about `<ORIGIN CITY>`. Fares on any route tend to climb further as departure approaches and popular evening slots fill first. If flying, book tonight.

**Reimbursement implication:** a single one-way fare at these last-minute prices can already exceed the entire Rs 5,000 team cap by itself, before the return leg or the second traveler is counted. If `<ORIGIN CITY>` turns out to require flying, budget for the cap to cover a small fraction of the trip, not the whole thing. Keep the airline's GST invoice/PDF receipt, not just the boarding pass.

### Road

**Journey shape:** highway driving (self-drive, a hired car, or a bus) directly into the GIFT City area. GIFT City is served by 4-6 lane state and national highways from all directions; the Ahmedabad-side approach via SG Highway is approximately 25 km from central Ahmedabad, 40-60 minutes depending on traffic (retrieved 2026-08-18).

**What to book:** for a hired car, book tonight through a known aggregator (Zoomcar, Revv, or a local outstation-cab operator) and get a written quote and invoice up front. For a bus, GSRTC and private operators run Ahmedabad/Gandhinagar to GIFT City; a GSRTC schedule specifically for this corridor exists (crm.giftgujarat.in/uploads/GSRTC.pdf, confirmed to exist but its exact timings could not be extracted from the file itself, retrieved 2026-08-18). Download it directly and cross-check departure times before relying on it.

**Booking window risk:** the lowest of the three for a hired car or self-drive (availability is rarely the binding constraint), but still confirm today if a specific vehicle or driver is needed for a Thursday evening slot. If self-driving a long distance, factor in a full day of driving fatigue arriving the night before a 22-hour sprint, which works against the entire point of arriving rested.

**Reimbursement implication:** self-drive costs (fuel, tolls) are the hardest to evidence cleanly. Keep every fuel receipt and the FASTag statement, photographed at the time, not reconstructed afterward. A hired car or bus ticket produces a cleaner single receipt.

### Last-mile transfer into GIFT City, and whether ride-hailing is reliable there

Uber and Ola both list Gandhinagar as an actively served city with 24/7 availability, per their own city pages (retrieved 2026-08-18). What no source confirms is how pickups behave once inside the GIFT City campus itself, a gated, security-controlled financial SEZ: whether an app-cab driver can pull up at a specific tower or hall, or whether there's a fixed pickup point at a gate, is **not verified**. Practical approach: do not assume an app cab will materialize instantly at the exact hall door. Confirm the pickup point by phone with the driver once a ride is accepted, and keep a few minutes of slack in any GIFT-City-internal transfer (the Friday hotel-to-venue leg in section 7 already builds this in).

## 4. What to book, in what order, tonight

1. **Hotel first.** It is the most availability-constrained item (three hotels, one overlapping expo, two days out) and the most likely to sell out completely rather than just get pricier. Lock the room before anything else.
2. **Outbound travel second**, using the mode decided in section 2 once `<ORIGIN CITY>` is known. Booked second because price rises with time but availability rarely disappears completely (an alternate flight or train class is almost always still biddable, even if not the cheapest one).
3. **Thursday-evening last-mile transfer third** (station or airport to hotel), booked or at minimum planned, so arrival night doesn't turn into a search for a working cab in the dark with two laptops and bags.
4. **Friday-morning hotel-to-venue transfer fourth**, planned per section 7. No need to pre-book if using an app cab, but know the plan tonight.
5. **Return travel last, and only partially.** Decide the mode (train vs flight vs road) for the return trip now, but hold off on the exact Saturday time slot until section 8's open question resolves. Locking a fixed return slot before closing time is confirmed risks either a missed non-refundable ticket or an unnecessary extra hotel night.

## 8. Saturday plan

Closing time is not confirmed in the brief ("Top 15 announced at closing, time TBC", `00_SITUATION_BRIEF.md`). What is known: coach review 12:00-14:00, Round 1 pitches 14:00-16:30, and if selected to the Top 20, Round 2 runs 17:00-19:00, with closing sometime after. Two cases:

**Case A: leave Saturday night.** Works if not selected past Round 1 (a clean exit around 16:30-17:00) or if Round 2 plus closing wraps at a reasonable hour. Risk: by Saturday evening you're running on the sprint's minimal sleep (one nap 02:00-04:30, the other 04:30-07:00 per the master plan, then a full day of pitching), and closing time is explicitly unconfirmed. A same-night departure booked rigidly in advance risks either missing a fixed slot if closing runs long, or a fatigued long journey if it doesn't.

**Case B: stay Saturday night, travel Sunday.** Removes the closing-time risk entirely, and the fatigue risk of travelling straight off a 30+ hour stretch. Costs a second hotel night not covered by the reimbursement cap (already earmarked for Thursday), and requires a room actually being available two days after the initial booking window.

**Recommendation:** do not lock in a fixed Saturday-night return ticket now. Decide the return leg's mode (per section 2) but leave the exact time slot open until Saturday afternoon, once the Top 20 cut (roughly 16:30-17:00) and, if applicable, Round 2's actual finish time are known. Separately, place a flexible or free-cancellation hold on a Saturday-night room at whichever hotel you used Thursday (ask at Thursday checkout whether they can hold a rate for Saturday), so Case B is available without a scramble if closing runs late. This costs nothing until confirmed, and removes the worst case: two exhausted founders with nowhere to sleep and no room left in the area.
