The problem, in one paragraph

A fund management entity in GIFT IFSC files a quarterly return 21 calendar days after quarter end, four times a year. The numbers on it arrive from several independent parties: the administrator, the internal ledger, the subscription register, the custodian, the valuer. Those parties close their books at different times and answer different questions, so they disagree, and nothing in the documents announces it. The Compliance Officer and the Principal Officer sign personally. Today the reasoning behind each number lives in an email thread, and when the officer leaves, it goes with them.

Every system built for this picks a number. A silent pick is the one error nobody downstream can detect, because there is no disagreement left to find. That is the failure this product exists to refuse.

How it works

Read it. Check it. Refuse to guess.

Six steps. Three use a model. Three are plain code, and the plain code ones are the ones that check. A model cannot check a model.

StageStepWhat it doesModel?
ReadScopeFinds which documents mention which fields, by matching the field name in the textNo
ExtractA model reads every number and quotes the line it came fromYes
CheckBindLooks for that exact quote in the document. Not there, character for character, and the number is thrown awayNo
ValidateChecks the arithmetic ties. Undrawn must equal committed minus drawnNo
CriticiseA second model, from a different company, tries to knock the reading downYes
RefuseReconcileDescribes the disagreement and its cause. Forbidden from choosing, in codeYes
A personDecides in writing, a second person signs off, the record is sealedHuman

The model that reads and the model that criticises are forced onto different companies, checked when the system starts rather than in review, so a model criticising its own reading is a startup failure and never a quiet one.

We removed a model on 19 August, and measured it. The scope step used to be a model. On a live run it read a document and returned two fields, silently dropping a third that was plainly on the page. We put deterministic string matching underneath it as a floor, then noticed the floor was overruling the model every time they disagreed, which means the model could only ever lose evidence. So we deleted it. Same corpus, before and after: 24 of 24 planted archetypes, zero silent picks, 299 tests passing. Nothing measurable was lost and one failure mode went with it. What we have not built, said before you find it

  • Per-field regulatory rule mapping is not built. It is stated as absent on screen. We will not invent a citation, and that is ask 4 of the residency.
  • The offline path is recorded responses plus deterministic matching, not a local model. The provider interface exists; the wiring does not.
  • We have never seen a real quarterly return. Every document here is fictional and labelled fictional on its face.
  • No dollar cost is quoted, because the account has no pricing API access, so any price would be recalled rather than sourced.
