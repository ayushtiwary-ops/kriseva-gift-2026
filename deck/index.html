Slide 2 · the problem

Four numbers, five sources, one personal signature

A fund management entity files a quarterly return 21 days after quarter end, four times a year. The numbers arrive from the administrator, the internal ledger, the subscription register, the custodian and the valuer. They close their books at different times and answer different questions.

So they disagree, and nothing in the documents announces it. The Compliance Officer and the Principal Officer sign personally.

Slide 3 · today

The reasoning lives in an email thread

The officer sees two numbers, cannot tell an error from a timing difference, emails the administrator, and the answer lives in that reply. Nothing records why the number that was filed was chosen.

A change of Compliance Officer is notified to IFSCA within 15 days. The records have to survive 8 years. The reasoning is the part that dies.

Slide 4 · what every system does instead

It picks a number, and nothing announces it

Given two figures, a normal system files the one most documents agree on. On our demo case that rule files the wrong figure for drawn capital, confidently, with no flag.

A silent pick is the only error nobody downstream can detect, because there is no disagreement left to find. That is the failure this product exists to refuse.

Slide 5 · what ATTEST does

It refuses, and shows the working

  • Every candidate number is bound to the exact line it came from, and any quote that cannot be located in the source is dropped.
  • Where sources disagree, the field is held open with every reading preserved and the cause named.
  • Every verdict on screen is clickable and opens the tests that ran, which fired, which were ruled out, and who stands behind each document.
  • A named human decides in writing, a second person signs off, and the record is sealed so an edit breaks the chain.
Slide 6 · architecture

Read it. Check it. Refuse to guess.

Six steps. Three use a model. Three are plain code, and the plain code ones are the ones that check.

Scope finds which documents mention which fields, by matching text. Extract reads the numbers and quotes the line. Bind goes and checks that quote is really there. Validate checks the arithmetic ties. Criticise puts a second model, from a different company, against the first. Reconcile describes the disagreement and is forbidden from choosing. Then a named person decides.

A model cannot check a model. The reader and the critic are forced onto different companies, and the system refuses to start if they are not.

Slide 7 · measured

24 of 24, and zero silent picks

24 / 24

Ten distinct failure archetypes across 24 synthetic cases. The right field named with exactly the planted cause, every time. Zero silent picks.

The ground truth is written into each case by deterministic code before any model sees it, so the system is not grading its own homework. We wrote the conflicts, which makes this a consistency check rather than an accuracy claim, and we say so.

Slide 8 · the honest slide

We predicted a frontier model would fail this. It did not

We ran the same documents through three frontier models, each told to answer UNCERTAIN when sources disagree. All three abstained correctly on every conflicted field, with zero silent picks.

One column separated them: evidence localisation, where one model could not produce a single quote that actually appeared in the source document. We are showing that table rather than the one we hoped for, because a measured result that surprises us is worth more than a predicted one that flatters us.

What no prompt produced: a named decider, a written reason, a second signature, or the same answer eleven months from now.

Slide 9 · what is not built

Said before you find it

  • Per-field regulatory rule mapping is not built. It says so on screen. We will not invent a citation.
  • The offline path is recorded responses, not a local model. The interface exists, the wiring does not.
  • We have never seen a real quarterly return. Every document is fictional and says so on its face.
  • Nine defects were found by using the product, all of which survived 299 passing tests. All nine are published.
